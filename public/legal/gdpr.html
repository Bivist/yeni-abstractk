This notice is intended for individuals in the European Economic Area and other locations where the GDPR applies to Abstract's processing. It describes the current application functions and must be completed after the controller's establishment and provider arrangements are verified.

1. Controller

Controller: Binali (individual publisher).
Contact details: A contact channel will be provided at a later date.
No representative or Data Protection Officer is identified in this draft. Whether an EU representative under Article 27 or a DPO is required must be assessed based on the controller's establishment, activities and scale of processing.

2. Personal data and purposes

Account data (email, Supabase user ID, display name and basic Google profile information where Google sign-in is used) is processed to create and manage accounts. Profile images are resized and stored in Supabase Storage. Saved papers, folders, reading history, reading streak and settings are stored locally and, for signed-in users, synchronized through Supabase. Search terms and filters are sent to OpenAlex to retrieve scholarly records. If you opt in, PostHog may receive app usage events. Session recording is disabled in the current configuration.

3. Legal bases

Depending on the purpose, processing may be necessary to perform a contract or take steps at your request, comply with a legal obligation, pursue legitimate interests where those interests are not overridden by your rights, or rely on consent for optional analytics. The controller must document the precise Article 6 basis for each purpose before launch; this draft does not make a blanket determination.

4. Recipients and international transfers

Potential service providers are Supabase, Google, OpenAlex and (only after analytics opt-in) PostHog. Their actual hosting locations, data-processing agreements, subprocessors and transfer instruments have not yet been verified. If personal data is transferred outside the EEA, the controller must identify and disclose the applicable GDPR Chapter V mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards under Article 46, where applicable. No specific safeguard is claimed by this draft.

5. Retention

Browser data remains until cleared or overwritten. Account and synchronized information is stored by the relevant providers. Exact retention periods, backup deletion cycles and a complete account/avatar deletion workflow require verification and documentation. Data should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority. Requests: A contact channel will be provided at a later date.

7. Automated decisions and children

Abstract is not currently designed to make legal or similarly significant decisions about users solely by automated processing. It is not designed specifically for children; age-related requirements must be reviewed for each target market.

8. Updates

This notice may be updated as the application and its providers change. The revision date appears above.

Publication warning: The contact details, controller establishment, Article 27 representative assessment, Article 6 legal bases, provider locations, processor agreements, international transfer safeguards and retention schedule must be completed before treating this notice as final.
